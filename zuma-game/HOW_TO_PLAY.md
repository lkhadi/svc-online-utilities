# Zuma Game - How to Play

## Quick Start

1. Click the **PLAY** button to start the game
2. Aim your cannon by moving the mouse or dragging on touch devices
3. Click or tap to shoot the colored ball

## Controls

### 🖱️ Mouse Controls
- **Aim**: Move mouse to point the cannon
- **Shoot**: Click left mouse button
- **Pause**: Click pause button

### 📱 Touch Controls
- **Aim**: Drag your finger to rotate the cannon
- **Shoot**: Tap anywhere to launch the ball
- **Pause**: Tap pause button

### ⌨️ Keyboard Controls
- **Arrow Keys**: Aim left/right/up/down
- **Space Bar**: Shoot the ball
- **Esc**: Pause the game

## Game Objectives

### 🎯 Main Goal
Pop 3 or more balls of the same color to eliminate them from the chain.

### 🛡️ Defense Goal
Prevent the balls from reaching the end of the path. If balls pass the end point, you lose a life.

### ⏱️ Time Goal
Complete each level before time runs out. You have 5 minutes per level.

## Scoring

### Basic Points
- **3 balls**: 10 points
- **4 balls**: 20 points
- **5 balls**: 30 points
- **6+ balls**: 40 points per ball

### Combo Bonuses
- **Combo x2**: 1.5x multiplier
- **Combo x3**: 2x multiplier
- **Combo x4**: 3x multiplier
- **Combo x5+**: 4x multiplier

### Level Bonuses
- Complete level within time limit
- Reach high scores in a level
- Chain multiple combos in one session

## Game Mechanics

### Ball Matching
- Look for consecutive balls of the same color
- They must be touching or connected by other balls of the same color
- Matches larger than 3 automatically trigger a chain reaction

### Chain Reactions
- When a combo is detected, the matched balls disappear
- This creates space in the chain
- New spaces may cause adjacent balls to shift together
- If adjacent balls form new matches, they're automatically eliminated

### Ball Growth
- When balls of the same color collide, they merge
- Merged balls become larger
- Larger balls pop more easily

### Path Movement
- Balls automatically follow the winding path
- They move at a steady pace
- The chain speeds up as more balls are added

### Shooter Mechanics
- The cannon always aims at your pointer/cursor
- Balls are shot at a constant speed
- After each shot, the cannon rotates back to the center
- Each shot has a small cooldown period

## Tips and Strategies

### 🎯 Aim Strategically
- Aim for gaps in the chain where you can create large matches
- Don't just shoot randomly - plan your shots
- Consider how balls will shift after a pop

### ⚡ Create Combos
- Try to create chain reactions
- More consecutive pops = more points
- Plan your shots to set up multiple matches

### 🛡️ Protect the End
- Keep an eye on the end of the path
- Don't let too many balls accumulate there
- Prioritize shots that clear the end area

### 🕐 Time Management
- Don't rush - precision is more important than speed
- Save shots for critical moments
- Sometimes it's better to let balls move closer before shooting

### 🎨 Color Strategy
- Remember which balls are coming
- Plan ahead based on the color sequence
- Sometimes it's better to let a color accumulate to create big combos

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ Not Planning Ahead
- Random shooting won't create chain reactions
- Think about where balls will move after a pop

### ❌ Letting Balls Accumulate at the End
- The end of the path is critical
- Clear balls there as soon as possible

### ❌ Ignoring Combos
- Combos are worth much more than single matches
- Look for opportunities to create multi-ball matches

### ❌ Rushing Your Shots
- Fast shooting leads to mistakes
- Quality over quantity

## Level Progression

### Starting Levels
- Basic paths
- Fewer balls
- Slower ball speed
- More time

### Intermediate Levels
- More complex paths
- More balls
- Faster ball speed
- Less time
- Added obstacles

### Advanced Levels
- Very complex paths
- Many balls
- Fast ball speed
- Short time limit
- Many obstacles

## Losing the Game

You lose a life when:
1. **Ball reaches end**: A ball passes the end of the path
2. **5 lives lost**: After 5 balls reach the end, game over

You lose the game when:
1. **No lives remaining**: When 5 balls have reached the end
2. **Time runs out**: You lose all points if time expires

## Winning the Game

You win when:
1. **All balls cleared**: Successfully pop all balls in the chain
2. **Level complete**: Pass each level within the time limit
