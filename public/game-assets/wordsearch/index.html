How to Play

1. Find Words: Swipe horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to connect letters and find the hidden words shown in the list.

2. Powerups:
✨ Magic: Reveals one full hidden word.
💡 Hint: Reveals the starting letter of a hidden word.
Aa Font: Changes the grid size.

3. Data Saving: Enable "Save Game Progress" in Settings to keep your level when you close the page.

4. Win: Find all words to complete the level and progress!
